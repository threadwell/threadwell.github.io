How to back up your text messages on Android

Threadwell saves your texts, picture messages and settings to one backup file that you keep. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose where to put the file.

Make a backup

  1. Tap Settings, then open Backup & data.
  2. Tap Back up everything.
  3. Leave Protect with a password (recommended) ticked, and type a password twice.
  4. Tap Choose where to save and pick where to keep the file, such as your Downloads folder. Copy it somewhere safe off the phone too.

What's in the backup

  • Your text messages and picture, video and audio messages, with their media.
  • Blocked numbers, Spaces, saved messages, notes and Space icons.
  • Your settings, theme pictures, spam history and future scheduled texts.
  • Not included: API keys you added, such as for AI image edits. Enter them again after restoring.

Write the password down. There's no way to recover a forgotten password, so nobody can restore the backup without it, including you. Without a password, anyone who opens the file can read your messages.

Restore on a new phone

Install Threadwell and set it as your texting app. It offers to restore a backup the first time. Later, use Settings → Backup & data → Restore from backup. Threadwell shows what's in the file, lets you choose what to bring back, and adds it to what's already on the phone without replacing anything. Then it restarts.
