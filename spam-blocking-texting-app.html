A texting app that blocks spam on your phone

Threadwell is an SMS and MMS app for Android. It replaces your phone's default messaging app and screens every text for spam before it reaches your inbox.

In short
  • Spam detection runs on your phone. Your messages are not uploaded anywhere to be checked.
  • Likely spam goes to a Spam folder. Texts that only look suspicious stay in your inbox with a warning.
  • You can add your own spam words, block numbers, and always allow senders you trust.
  • Saved contacts are never moved to Spam automatically.

How Threadwell stops spam texts

Every incoming text gets a score from 0 to 100. The score comes from several checks that run on the phone:

  • An on-device spam model that reads the wording of the message. It counts most for unknown 10-digit numbers, where scam texts usually come from, and much less for business short codes, so your bank and delivery alerts aren't caught by mistake.
  • Suspicious links, such as links to raw IP addresses, domains often used for scams, phishing phrases, or a link from someone who isn't in your contacts.
  • Your own spam keywords. Add a word or phrase in Settings and any text containing it gets flagged.
  • A shared list of known spam (domains, senders and phrases), downloaded once a day from this website and checked on the phone.

A high score moves the conversation to the Spam folder. A middle score keeps it in your inbox and shows a banner that says This might be spam, with Always allow and Restore once.

A Spam folder you can actually review

Spam blockers that silently delete texts can lose the one message you were waiting for. Threadwell keeps flagged texts where you can see them:

  • The Spam folder groups texts by kind: delivery scams, prize and reward scams, phishing, loans, political and survey texts, unknown links, and ones you marked yourself.
  • Not spam and Restore bring a conversation back, and Threadwell learns from your choice.
  • Delete a whole group at once, or have spam deleted automatically after 7 or 30 days.
  • Spam statistics show what was caught over time.

Block numbers and report spam texts

  • Block a sender and their texts are dropped silently. Threadwell uses Android's own blocked numbers list, so the block applies to calls too.
  • Report to 7726, the carrier spam reporting number, is off by default. Turn it on and Threadwell forwards high-scoring spam for you, or report a single message yourself. Each report is an ordinary text on your plan.
  • Report to the FTC copies the message details and opens the US Federal Trade Commission's report form.

Private by design

Threadwell has no accounts, no analytics and no developer servers. Spam checks happen on the phone; the only spam-related download is the shared rules list, and nothing about your messages is sent with it. The app privacy policy lists every network connection the app can make.

More than a spam filter

Threadwell is a complete texting app: SMS, picture and video messages, group texts, scheduled send, dual SIM, pinned chats and message categories. It also adds Spaces for plans worth keeping, checklists and reminders to come back to a conversation. See how it compares with Google Messages.

Questions

Does Threadwell block spam calls?

No. Threadwell is a texting app. Numbers you block in Threadwell go on Android's blocked list, which your phone app also uses.

Will it hide texts from my contacts?

No. A text from a saved contact can get a warning banner, but it is never moved to Spam on its own.

Does it work with RCS chats?

No. Threadwell sends and receives SMS and MMS. RCS chat messages are handled by a separate system on the phone and don't reach other texting apps.

Where can I get it?

Threadwell is in a closed test on Google Play before it opens to everyone. Become a tester, or try the spam rules demo in your browser.
