How to block spam texts on Android

Threadwell screens every text on your phone and moves likely spam to a Spam folder. You can also block a sender yourself and add your own spam rules.

Block a number that sends spam

  1. Open the conversation from the spam sender.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right and choose Block & report spam.
  3. Tap Block to confirm. You won't get texts from that number, and the conversation moves to Spam.

You can also press and hold the conversation in your inbox and choose Block sender. To undo a block, go to Settings → Spam protection → Blocked numbers. Threadwell uses Android's blocked numbers list, so the block also applies in your phone app.

Stop spam texts with your own keywords

  1. Tap Settings, then open Spam protection.
  2. Under Spam keywords, type a word or phrase and tap Add.
  3. New texts that contain it are flagged. A text that looks suspicious shows a banner, This might be spam, with Always allow and Restore once.

Turn on automatic spam detection

In Settings → Spam protection, keep Smart detection and Flag suspicious links on. Threadwell scores each incoming text on your phone and moves likely spam to the Spam folder. Saved contacts are never moved to Spam on their own.

To report spam to your carrier, turn on Automatically report detected spam to 7726. It's off by default, and each report is an ordinary text on your plan.

Got a real text marked as spam?

Open the Spam folder, open the conversation, and choose Restore to inbox. Threadwell learns from your choice.
