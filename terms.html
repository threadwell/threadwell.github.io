Threadwell terms of service

Last updated 13 November 2026. These terms cover the Threadwell Android app. How the app handles your data is described in the app privacy policy.

In short
  • Texting with Threadwell is free. Threadwell Plus is an optional yearly subscription. There is no account to create.
  • Your messages stay on your phone. You are responsible for what you send.
  • The app is provided as is. Keep your own backups of anything important.

Using Threadwell

By installing or using Threadwell you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app. You may use Threadwell for your own personal messaging, on any device you own or are allowed to use.

Your messages and your carrier

  • Threadwell works as your phone's default SMS app. Texts and picture messages are sent and received through your mobile carrier, on your own plan. Any charges from your carrier are between you and them.
  • Delivery depends on your carrier and the recipient's phone. Threadwell can't guarantee that a message is delivered, delivered on time, or delivered at all.
  • Threadwell is not for emergencies. To reach emergency services, call them directly.
  • You are responsible for what you send and who you send it to. Don't use Threadwell to send spam, to harass anyone, or to break the law.

Optional features and outside services

Some optional features use outside services, such as weather, sports and stock widgets, link previews, AI image edits with your own key, and photo backdrops. Each is listed in the app privacy policy, and each is off until you turn it on. Those services have their own terms, and they can change or stop working at any time. If you add your own key for a service, its terms apply to how you use it.

Threadwell Plus

Threadwell Plus is an optional subscription that adds smart spam detection, the spam rule feed and the animated scene themes. Texting, picture messages, your keyword list, blocking and the colour themes stay free whether or not you subscribe.

  • Threadwell Plus is sold through Google Play and billed yearly at the price shown in Google Play before you buy. Google Play handles payment, taxes and receipts.
  • It renews automatically each year until you cancel. Cancel any time in Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; you keep Plus until the end of the year you paid for.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. You can also email support.
  • If the subscription ends, the Plus features turn off. Your messages, Spaces and settings are kept.

Spam protection

Spam detection runs on your phone and makes its best guess. It can miss spam, and it can sometimes flag a real message. Check the Spam folder if you're expecting something. Reporting to 7726 sends an ordinary text message on your plan, and only happens when you turn it on or report a message yourself.
