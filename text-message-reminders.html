How to get reminded to reply to a text

Read a text at a bad moment and meant to answer later? Mark it with Come back to this and Threadwell reminds you.

Set a reminder

  1. Save the message to a Space (see how to save a text).
  2. Open Browse Spaces and the Space that holds it.
  3. On the saved message, tap Come back to this.
  4. Pick a time, such as Tomorrow morning, and confirm.

When the reminder comes

You get a notification at the time you picked, with Complete and Snooze. Everything you're waiting to come back to is listed in Threads to Tend, under My Spaces. Tap Complete when you're done; the message stays saved in its Space.

Reminders are notifications, so notifications need to be allowed for Threadwell in Android settings. Nothing is ever sent as a text.
