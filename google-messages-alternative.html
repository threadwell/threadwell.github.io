An alternative to Google Messages

Most Android phones come with Google Messages or the phone maker's own texting app. Threadwell is a different texting app you can set as your default instead. Here is what changes, including what you give up.

In short
  • Threadwell sends and receives regular texts, picture and video messages, and group texts (SMS and MMS) on your own carrier plan.
  • Spam detection runs on your phone, with a Spam folder you can review and rules you control.
  • It adds places to keep plans, checklists and reminders that come out of your conversations.
  • No account, no analytics, no cloud copy of your messages.
  • It does not do RCS chat, so typing indicators, read receipts and chat over Wi-Fi aren't available.

What Threadwell adds

Spam protection you can see

Every incoming text is scored on the phone. Likely spam moves to a Spam folder, grouped by kind (delivery scams, prize scams, phishing and more), and texts that only look suspicious stay in your inbox with a warning. Add your own spam words, always allow senders you trust, and optionally forward spam to 7726. How spam blocking works.

Spaces for the things worth keeping

Save a message from any conversation into a Space, such as "Weekend away" or "Kitchen remodel", so the cabin booking and the contractor's quote don't get buried. Turn a saved message into a checklist, and give each Space its own icon and color.

Reminders to come back to a conversation

Mark a message with Come back to this and pick a time. It shows up in Threads to Tend, and you get a notification with Complete and Snooze.

Two ways to see your messages

Keep a familiar inbox, or switch to Threadwell view: your conversations, Spaces and saved items side by side, which works especially well on a tablet or an unfolded foldable.

Everyday texting extras

  • Scheduled send, drafts and a signature.
  • Pinned chats, mute and archive.
  • Dual SIM: choose which SIM sends, per conversation.
  • Copy one-time codes straight from the notification.
  • Photo groups shown as a stack, carousel or ring.
  • Themes, from calm colors to animated scenes.
  • A full backup to a file you keep, with an optional password.

Coming in the next update: message categories (Personal, Money, Codes, Updates, Promotions), pinned chats in your own order, and jumping to any date in a long conversation.

What you give up

  • RCS chat. Threadwell sends everything as SMS and MMS. RCS chats are handled by a separate system on the phone that other texting apps can't reach.
  • Texting from a computer. Threadwell has no web or desktop version.
  • Cloud sync. Your messages stay on your phone. Use the backup file to move to a new phone.

Switching is easy to undo

When you open Threadwell it asks to become your default texting app. Your existing messages are stored by Android, not by the app, so they're all there. To switch back, change the default SMS app in Android settings. Nothing is lost either way.

Get Threadwell

Threadwell is in a closed test on Google Play before it opens to everyone. Become a tester, or try the playground in your browser first. It runs on sample conversations and sends nothing.
