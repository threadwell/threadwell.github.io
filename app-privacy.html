Threadwell app privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026. This policy covers the Threadwell Android app. For this website, see the website privacy page.

In short
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads and no developer-run servers.
  • Your messages and contacts stay on your phone and are never sent to the developer.
  • Anything that does reach the internet is listed below, and the optional parts are off until you turn them on.

What the app handles on your phone

  • Messages and contacts. Threadwell is a text messaging app. It reads and writes Android's SMS and MMS storage and reads your contacts, on your device only, to show and send conversations. Spam scoring runs on your device.
  • Phone state and number. Optionally read to label SIMs and to recognize your own number in group threads.
  • Calendar. Read on your device only if you use a calendar widget, to show your events.
  • Location. Only if you choose to share your location in a conversation, or turn on Use my location for the Weather theme or widget. A shared location goes to the person you choose. Weather uses approximate coordinates.
  • App data. Settings, drafts, scheduled messages, the picture message retry queue, Spaces and notes, and spam statistics are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

These services have their own privacy practices. The app has no analytics, no ad software and no remote crash reporting.

Your keys

Keys you add for optional features (Gemini, Unsplash) are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never included in backups or phone to phone transfers.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Threadwell sends nothing to cloud backup. If you move to a new phone with Android's direct phone to phone transfer, no server is involved, and these come along: your settings, drafts, spam history and learning, your Spaces and notes, and the small private copies of any photos you chose as Space icons. Keys, scheduled messages and the retry queue never do. Your messages themselves follow Android's own text message backup, not Threadwell's.

Threadwell can also create a backup file or a text export, but only when you ask, and it saves it where you choose (exports go to Downloads/Threadwell). These files contain your messages, so keep them somewhere safe. If a phone is lost or wiped and there is no second device, Threadwell's own settings and Spaces are lost with it, so use the backup file if you want a copy.

Sharing and temporary copies

When you share a photo, Threadwell makes a temporary copy so another app can read it. These copies are cleared automatically after about an hour, and you can clear them yourself.

Logs and crash reports

Threadwell does not log message text. Crash reports are created on your phone, with quoted text and phone numbers removed, and stay there unless you choose to share one. If you send a bug report, it includes basic device information (app version, Android version and device model).

Your choices

  • Everything optional above is controlled in the app, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • In Settings → Privacy & data you can clear downloaded data (previews, widget caches, shared files) and delete crash reports. Clearing caches never touches your messages, Spaces or notes.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private data. Backup files and exports you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.

Sharing and retention

The developer collects nothing and shares nothing. Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Spam statistics are removed automatically as they age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
