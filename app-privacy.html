Threadwell app privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. This policy covers the Threadwell Android app, published on Google Play by Threadwell (“the developer” below). For this website, see the website privacy page.

In short
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads and no developer-run servers.
  • Your messages and contacts stay on your phone and are never sent to the developer.
  • Anything that does reach the internet is listed below, and the optional parts are off until you turn them on.
  • There is no account, so there is nothing to delete on a server. How to delete your data explains what to remove on your phone.

What the app handles on your phone

  • Messages and contacts. Threadwell is a text messaging app. It reads and writes Android's SMS and MMS storage and reads your contacts, on your device only, to show and send conversations. Spam scoring runs on your device.
  • Phone state and number. Optionally read to label SIMs and to recognize your own number in group threads.
  • Calendar. Read on your device only if you turn on the Calendar widget (read only), to show your events, and for the Commute widget if you let it use your next event's location.
  • Location. Only if you choose to share your location in a conversation, or turn on Use my location for the Weather theme or widget. A shared location goes to the person you choose, as a text message. If you share your live location by text, Threadwell shows a notification while it runs and sends an updated link to that conversation every few minutes until you stop it. Weather uses approximate coordinates.
  • Notifications. Used to tell you about new messages, reminders and scheduled sends.
  • App data. Settings, drafts, scheduled messages, the picture message retry queue, Spaces and notes, and spam statistics are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

These services have their own privacy practices. The app has no analytics, no ad software and no remote crash reporting.

Threadwell Plus

Threadwell Plus is an optional yearly subscription sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under Google's privacy policy, and Threadwell never sees your card details. The app keeps only a note on your phone of whether you are subscribed. Google gives developers order records for the sales it processes, which the developer uses only for refunds, support and tax. Texting never depends on a subscription.

Permissions the app asks for

  • SMS and MMS (send, receive, read, write): to work as your default texting app. Android only lets the default texting app save messages, so Threadwell can't work without them.
  • Contacts: to show names and photos instead of numbers.
  • Phone (optional): to label SIMs on dual-SIM phones and recognize your own number.
  • Notifications: for new messages and reminders.
  • Location (optional): only when you share your location or use your location for weather.
  • Calendar (optional, read only): only for the Calendar and Commute widgets.
  • Run at startup: to put scheduled messages, reminders and picture message retries back after the phone restarts.

Your keys

Keys you add for optional features (Gemini, Unsplash) are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never included in backups or phone to phone transfers.

Backups and moving to a new phone

Threadwell sends nothing to cloud backup. If you move to a new phone with Android's direct phone to phone transfer, no server is involved, and these come along: your settings, drafts, spam history and learning, your Spaces and notes, and the small private copies of any photos you chose as Space icons. Keys, scheduled messages and the retry queue never do. Your messages themselves follow Android's own text message backup, not Threadwell's.

Threadwell can also create a backup file or a text export, but only when you ask, and it saves it where you choose (exports go to Downloads/Threadwell). These files contain your messages, so keep them somewhere safe. If a phone is lost or wiped and there is no second device, Threadwell's own settings and Spaces are lost with it, so use the backup file if you want a copy.

Sharing and temporary copies

When you share a photo, Threadwell makes a temporary copy so another app can read it. These copies are cleared automatically after about an hour, and you can clear them yourself.

Logs and crash reports

Threadwell does not log message text. Crash reports are created on your phone, with quoted text and phone numbers removed, and stay there unless you choose to share one. If you send a bug report, it includes basic device information (app version, Android version and device model).

Your choices

  • Everything optional above is controlled in the app, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • In Settings → Privacy & data you can clear downloaded data (previews, widget caches, shared files) and delete crash reports. Clearing caches never touches your messages, Spaces or notes.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its private data. Backup files and exports you saved yourself stay where you put them until you delete them.

How to delete your data

Threadwell has no accounts and no servers, so the developer holds no copy of your data to delete. Everything is on your phone, and you can remove it yourself:

  • Threadwell's own data (settings, drafts, Spaces, notes, saved items, spam history and learning, keys, scheduled messages): open Settings → Privacy & data → Open app info, then Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Your messages are kept in Android's shared message storage, not inside Threadwell, so they stay on the phone if you uninstall. To delete them, long-press a conversation and choose Move to Trash, then open Trash and choose Delete forever (Trash also deletes conversations by itself after 30 days). Do this before clearing storage or uninstalling: a conversation that is only in Trash comes back if Threadwell's own data is cleared.
  • Backup files and exports you made stay wherever you saved them. Delete them in your file manager.
  • Messages already sent, including spam reports to 7726, have reached your carrier and the recipient and can't be recalled.
  • A Threadwell Plus subscription is managed in Google Play: cancel it under Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Your purchase history is kept by Google.

If you have emailed support and want those emails deleted, write to the address below and they will be deleted.

Children

Threadwell is not directed at children under 13.

Sharing and retention

The developer collects nothing from the app and shares nothing. Data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Spam statistics are removed automatically as they age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
