How to save important text messages

Addresses, booking details, gift ideas: the texts worth keeping get buried fast. Threadwell lets you save them into Spaces, your own folders that collect messages from any conversation.

Save a message to a Space

  1. Press and hold a message.
  2. In the action bar, tap More message actions and choose Add to a Space.
  3. Tick a Space, then tap Save. To make a new one, tap ＋ New Space in the same dialog.
  4. Later, open Browse Spaces and the Space to find it. Tap Show in chat to jump back to the original message.

Saving is part of Threadwell view, which you can switch on in Settings → Messaging & notifications → Layout. The message stays in its conversation; the Space keeps a link to it.

Make a Space

  1. In a conversation, open the Space list and tap ＋ New / manage Spaces, then ＋ New Space.
  2. Start blank or from a template, such as Travel or Gift ideas.
  3. Give it a name and a color, and tap Save. You can keep up to 20 Spaces.

Want to try it first? Play the interactive demo in your browser.
