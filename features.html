What Threadwell does

Updated November 2026, for version 0.2.5, now with testers on Google Play.

Threadwell is an SMS and MMS texting app for Android. It becomes your default texting app, works with your own carrier plan, and needs no account. Your messages stay on your phone.

New in this version
  • Message categories open as their own page, with search and bulk actions.
  • Spaces, Thread insights and the Links, Attachments and Important tabs now work in the traditional inbox too.
  • Press and hold to drag pinned chats into your own order.
  • Delete several messages at once, plus underline and text effects.
  • Lock a conversation and choose the sending SIM from either view.
  • Link previews explain why one couldn't load, with Try again.
  • Safer retries for scheduled and failed messages.

Two ways to see your messages

Keep a familiar inbox (the traditional view), or switch to Threadwell view, where your conversations, Spaces and saved items sit side by side. Threadwell view works especially well on a tablet or an unfolded foldable. Change it any time in Settings → Messaging & notifications → Layout.

The two views share the same features. In this version Spaces, Thread insights, Move to category, conversation locks and Send from are available in both. See both views.

Keep things organized

Message categories New page

Turn on Message categories and tabs appear above your conversations: Personal, Money, Codes, Updates and Promotions. Sorting happens on your phone, from who sent the message and what the latest one says. Saved contacts and group chats stay in Personal. You can make your own tabs, such as Work or Family, with sorting words, and move any conversation with Move to category.

Each category now opens as its own page. Search the list, or tap Select to mark read, mark unread, move, archive or move to Trash in one go. Categories are off by default. Try it.

Pinned chats, in your order New

Pin the conversations you open most. Keep them sorted by the newest message, or choose Arrange pinned chats and press and hold a chat to drag it where you want it (the up and down buttons still work too). Pins can show as rows or as a row of photos. Favorites has been folded into Pinned: if you had favorites, one tap on Pin these instead moves them over. Try it.

Spaces and Threadmarks

Save the part of a conversation worth keeping into a Space, such as "Weekend away", next to its checklist and notes. Mark something Come back to this and it waits in Threads to Tend with a reminder. Spaces now open from the traditional inbox too, for one conversation or all of them. Try it.

Links, Attachments and Important Both views

Every conversation has tabs for every shared link, every photo and file, and the messages you marked Important. Tap an Important message to open it in the conversation. Thread insights shows a timeline, who's talking, messages per day and the busiest time of day.

Find it again

  • Search with sections for Messages, Photos, Videos and Links.
  • Go to the first message, or to any date, in a long conversation. Try it.
  • Mute, archive, and a Trash you can restore from.

Write and send

Formatting and text effects New

Select text in the box and the formatting bar offers bold, italic, strikethrough and, new, underline. Texts are plain text, so the marks (*bold*, _italic_, ~strike~, __underline__) travel as you typed them and other phones see the marks.

Text effects (Big, Small, Shake, Nod, Ripple, Bloom and Jitter) are shown on your phone only. Whisper and Shout let you drag the Send button up or down to send a message bigger or smaller on your screen. Screen effects play balloons, confetti or fireworks for a fresh "Happy birthday", "Congratulations" or "Happy New Year", and can be turned off.

Select several messages Delete added

Press and hold a message and choose Select messages. Copy them, forward them, save them to a Space, or delete them. Deleting asks first, because it can't be undone.

Send on your terms

  • Undo send, with Edit to change a text before it goes out.
  • Scheduled send. If a scheduled or undo-able send was interrupted, Threadwell waits for you to check before trying again, so nothing goes out twice by surprise.
  • When it's unclear whether a picture message went through, it says Send status unknown instead of sending it again.
  • Edit a photo or video in your phone's editor before sending. Long videos are shrunk to fit, or split into a few texts.
  • Drafts, a signature, @mentions in groups and tapback reactions.

Choose which SIM sends Both views

On a dual-SIM phone, pick Send from for each conversation, from the conversation menu or the Threadwell view menu. If the SIM you chose has been removed or turned off, Threadwell never switches to another one quietly. It asks you, and your draft is kept.

Link previews, only if you want them

Link previews are off by default. If you turn them on, your phone fetches the title and image straight from the linked website. There is no Threadwell server in between, but the website you link to can see your phone opened it. Previews are skipped for messages flagged as spam, and now say why one couldn't load, with Try again where that can help. Links to a photo show the photo.

Send a large file (test)

Send a big video or file to another phone running Threadwell, directly from phone to phone, with no server or relay. It's a test feature, and both people turn on receiving in Settings first.

Privacy and locks

App lock

In Settings → Lock & notification privacy, turn on Lock Threadwell to ask for a PIN, or a fingerprint or face on Android 10 and newer. Choose how long Threadwell can sit in the background before it asks again. It always asks after a restart.

Private notifications

Choose what a new-message notification shows:

  • Show sender and message.
  • Show sender only. Reply still works.
  • Hide sender and message. Notifications only say "New message".

Lock a single conversation Both views

With app lock on, you can lock one conversation with the same PIN. Its preview reads "Locked conversation", it's left out of search, and its notifications show no sender or message. The lock also covers any duplicate copies of the same conversation. Lock or remove a lock from the conversation menu in either view.

Local first

No account, no analytics and no cloud copy of your messages. Back up everything to a file you keep, with an optional password. Read the app privacy policy for every connection the app can make.

Spam protection

Every incoming text is checked on your phone. Likely spam waits in the Spam folder, grouped into tabs like Delivery scam, Prize / reward and Phishing. Texts that only look suspicious stay in your inbox with a warning.

  • Not spam moves a conversation back to your inbox. New messages from it are still checked.
  • Always allow stops checking that conversation.
  • The Spam screens offer the same choices, in the same order, in both views.
  • Your own spam words, blocked numbers and an allow list.
  • Forwarding spam to 7726 is optional and off by default.

Look and feel

  • Themes, from calm colors to animated scenes, each with a full preview before you apply it. Try it.
  • Photo groups as a stack, cascade, list, carousel or ring.
  • Optional in-app widgets such as weather, calendar and countdowns. Try it.

About and updates

Settings → About Threadwell shows your version and where it was installed from. Check for updates runs only when you tap it, and asks the Google Play app on your phone. Threadwell never nags you to update or to rate it.

Get Threadwell

Threadwell is in a closed test on Google Play before it opens to everyone. Become a tester, or try the playground in your browser first. It runs on sample conversations and sends nothing.
