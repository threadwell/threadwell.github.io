Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026. This page covers this help website only.

What this site collects

Nothing. The site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, embedded videos, social media widgets or sign in. The interactive demos run entirely in your browser and never send what you tap or type anywhere.

The one thing that can be saved on your device is the playground, and only if you ask for it. See The playground below.

What loads when you visit

  • The page, its styles, scripts and images, all served from this site. Nothing is loaded from other companies, and the site uses the fonts already on your device.
  • Your browser may keep a normal cache of those files so the site loads faster next time. You can clear it in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. That processing is described in GitHub's own privacy statement. This site does not receive or use that information.

The demos use made up data

The names, messages and photos in the phone preview are samples. They are not real conversations. Tutorials keep nothing once you leave the page.

The playground

Explore Threadwell lets you create sample Spaces, notes, checklists and settings. By default these live only in your browser tab’s memory and disappear when you close or reload the tab. Nothing you write there is sent anywhere, including the messages you type in the sample conversations.

If you tick Remember this playground on this browser, a copy of that sample content is saved in your browser’s local storage on this device, so it is there next time. It is never uploaded. Untick the box, or choose Erase saved copy, to delete it, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Do not type anything you would not want kept on a shared computer while that box is ticked.

The Threadwell app

This page is not the privacy policy for the Threadwell app. Read the Threadwell app privacy policy, or find the same summary inside the app under Settings → Privacy & data.

Questions

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
