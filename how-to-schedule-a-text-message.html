How to schedule a text message on Android

Write a text now and let Threadwell send it later: a birthday message at midnight, or a reminder for the morning.

Schedule a text

  1. Open the conversation and type your message.
  2. Tap the ＋ (Add attachment) button next to the message box.
  3. Choose Schedule send.
  4. Pick a date, then a time. Threadwell confirms with Scheduled for and the time you chose.

Photos and other attachments can be scheduled too. Pick a time in the future.

See or cancel scheduled texts

Go to Settings → Messaging & notifications → Scheduled messages. Each one shows who it's for and when it will send, with a Cancel button.

Good to know

  • Your phone sends the text, so it needs to be on with signal at that time. If it's off, the message goes out once it can.
  • Android may send it a few minutes after the time you picked, to save battery.
  • Scheduled texts survive a restart.
